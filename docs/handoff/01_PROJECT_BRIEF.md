# 01 — Project Brief

**Identity:** *We are the place where Amazon deals other people can't place go to live.*

## The niche
Merchants Amazon pays and who can't leave: **Amazon FBA/FBM sellers, Amazon Relay carriers, Amazon DSP operators.** One-line qualifier for partners: *"Does Amazon pay them? Send the file."*

## The partners we recruit (brokers-first, by speed)
- **Tier A (first — speed):** MCA brokers / ISOs, business-credit consultants, loan brokers, tax-relief/credit-repair agents. They understand commissions and hold unplaced Amazon files now.
- **Tier B (second — volume):** Amazon agencies (PPC/listing/reimbursement), prep centers, 3PLs, inventory consultants, coaches, software affiliates, Relay dispatchers, factoring brokers who decline Amazon files.
- **Tier C (third — quality, slow):** e-commerce accountants/bookkeepers.

## The offer to partners
- **Certification:** *ICC Certified Amazon Deal Partner* — a status credential (badge, profile, referral link), not just training.
- **Commission:** 10–12% of the funded amount, **paid on renewals at the full rate**, protected merchant + non-circumvention (operator's existing ISO agreement). **No clawbacks on deals ≤ $10,000.**
- **Hook:** the **Deal Rescue Challenge** — *"Have an Amazon deal nobody could place? Let us look at it."*

## The engine in plain terms
1. **They come in file-first:** a partner uploads a deal via the Rescue Challenge → a provisional partner is created → a rules-only checker says *likely fundable / needs review / out of box* → the file routes to a VA queue, then into the operator's existing underwriting. E-sign of the ISO agreement is required before commission is payable; certification happens in parallel.
2. **They get found / reached via owned channels only:** a VA-built public seed list + automated enrichment; Tier A worked by VA phone/LinkedIn, Tier B/C by automated email from owned domains; inbound via the operator's own SEO/content + the referral loop.
3. **They stay engaged:** automatic status updates on every submission, a partner portal showing files/commissions/rank/referral link.
4. **The network compounds:** every funded deal generates referral asks (Phase 3+).

## The work split
- **Software:** intake, certification, e-sign, routing, status updates, commission ledger, (Phase 3+) sourcing/outreach/boards/viral loop.
- **VA:** seed-list building, Tier A phone/LinkedIn outreach, reply/exception/first-deal review queue.
- **Operator (~15 min/day):** approve funded files; mark fundings (which triggers commission + later the viral loop).
